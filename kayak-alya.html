Overview

Al Alya Hotel offers a comfortable setting when in Medina. It also provides 24-hour room service, a free shuttle service and free Wi-Fi.

The property offers a concierge, an express check-in and check-out feature and a 24-hour reception. A currency exchange, an atm/cash machine and a coffee bar are also provided.

Every comfortable apartment at Al Alya Hotel offers a refrigerator, as well as all the essentials for an enjoyable stay.

The property provides easy access to King Salman International Convention Center and Al-Baqi'. Al-Masjid Al-Nabawi is a brief stroll away.
